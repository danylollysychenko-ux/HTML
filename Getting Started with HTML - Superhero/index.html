Spooder Dude

About Spooder Dude:

Powers and Abilities

His strengths are:

Backstory

He got his proficiency in languages in college. The crazy strength he got from drinking a potion
given from a mysterious dude in the corner of the street. He bought the potion for about 100 dollars.
The stranger did mention there would be side affects if he drinks the potion but he did it anyway.
After the potion he gained his strength but after he drank the potion he finally noticed the side affects.
Which included not being able to lift below 215 pounds, can't get any friend and can't get a girlfriend.

Hero Life

Mission and Impact

He was able to easily lift the cars back up after a car accident and he is able to cook anything that exists
including new meals that might be made. He can cause accidental harm if he isn't careful with lifting the cars.
Spooder Dude is on the mission to help make the world a safe and better place.
He is also able to help translate for people that need it.

His biggest accomplishments are:

1. Cooked for some of the biggest restaurants in the world.

2. Lifted up 2500 cars in total that were in accidents.

3. Was able to be a translator for a lot of people that needed help.

Final Thoughts

The world does need Spooder Dude because he can help with car accidents, cooking and translating.
The world benefits a lot by having Spooder Dude help with what he is strong in.
Overall this hero, even with all his weaknesses can still impacts the world by using his strengths and skills.
