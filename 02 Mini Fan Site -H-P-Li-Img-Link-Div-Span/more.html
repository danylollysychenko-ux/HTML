How did I get into the game?

My dad getting me into the game

I got in to the game when I was about 9 years old.
My dad showed me other games that he used to play like starcraft 1 and warcraft 2.
I really loved this game, this is where I learned to try again after failing a mission objective.
The story that it has is probably better than some of the newer games that were realeased.
Whoever says this game is bad is wrong and should fully try it.

Alliance Campaign — Curse of the Blood Elves

Undead Campaign — Path of the Damned
